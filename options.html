Default behavior

These defaults apply everywhere except disabled sites.

Notes 1/5 local
Notes stay local and rotate across detected surfaces. Leave them all empty and nothing is drawn — detected surfaces are removed and the page closes over them.

Display

Theme for this extension's popup and settings. The in-page card takes its own tone from the site it lands on.

Blocking is separate

Network blocking stays on whenever Notturn is enabled, with or without notes. Use the global or per-site switch to pause it.

Disabled sites

One domain per line. Subdomains are included automatically.

Advanced Inspection and diagnostic guidance +

Missed something?

Open the extension on that page and choose Inspect missed clutter. The inspector shows what the detector sees and can save a compact report.

Detection remains conservative around forms, navigation, editors, and sensitive pages.
